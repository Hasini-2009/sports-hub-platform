# Acute Soft-Tissue Injury Management: PEACE & LOVE Protocol

The traditional RICE protocol (Rest, Ice, Compression, Elevation) has been largely replaced in evidence-based sports medicine by the British Journal of Sports Medicine (BJSM) **PEACE & LOVE** paradigm.

## Immediate Phase (First 24-72 hours) - PEACE
- **P - Protect**: Unload and restrict movement for the first 1-3 days to minimize bleeding and reduce risk of aggravating the lesion. Avoid prolonged immobilization.
- **E - Elevate**: Keep the injured limb higher than the heart to promote interstitial fluid drainage out of tissues.
- **A - Avoid Anti-inflammatory Modalities**: Do NOT use NSAIDs (ibuprofen) or excessive icing during the initial acute phase. Inflammation is necessary for collagen synthesis and tissue regeneration.
- **C - Compress**: Use elastic bandages or taping to limit intra-articular edema and tissue hemorrhage.
- **E - Educate**: Active recovery over passive modalities. Understand body signals and avoid unnecessary medical tests for minor sprains.

## Sub-Acute Phase (After Day 3) - LOVE
- **L - Load**: Active approach with movement and exercise benefits most patients. Mechanical stress should be added early without causing pain.
- **O - Optimism**: The brain plays a major role in pain perception. Confident expectations correlate with faster recovery.
- **V - Vascularization**: Pain-free cardiovascular physical activity (stationary biking, upper body ergometer) stimulates blood flow to repairing tissues.
- **E - Exercise**: Restore mobility, strength, and proprioception early to prevent recurrent sprains.

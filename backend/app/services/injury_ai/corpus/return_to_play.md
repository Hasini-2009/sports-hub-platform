# Return to Play (RTP) Progression Framework

Returning an athlete to competitive competition requires a multi-criteria progression continuum balancing biological tissue healing with functional athletic readiness.

## The 5-Stage RTP Continuum
1. **Stage 1 - Tissue Healing and Local Conditioning**: Protect repair site while maintaining non-affected limb and cardiovascular fitness. Pain score <= 2/10 during baseline daily living activities.
2. **Stage 2 - Load Tolerance and Kinetic Chain Symmetry**: Progressive resistance training. Limb Symmetry Index (LSI) >80% on isokinetic/dynamometer strength testing.
3. **Stage 3 - Sport-Specific Non-Contact Drills**: Deceleration mechanics, cutting, jumping, change of direction drills at 75-90% velocity without reactive external triggers.
4. **Stage 4 - Controlled Contact Practice**: Full team training drills, simulated pressure, tactical decision making under sport-induced fatigue.
5. **Stage 5 - Unrestricted Competitive Match Play**: Cleared by medical staff, physical therapist, and athlete psychological readiness confirmed.

## Psychological Readiness: The ACL-RSI and TSK-11
Physical healing does not guarantee functional performance. Athletes with high kinesiophobia (fear of movement or re-injury) exhibit compensatory movement patterns that increase re-injury rates.
- Administer the Anterior Cruciate Ligament Return to Sport after Injury (ACL-RSI) scale or Tampa Scale for Kinesiophobia (TSK-11).
- Target ACL-RSI score >60-70 before full contact competition.

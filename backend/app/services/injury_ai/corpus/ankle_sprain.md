# Acute Lateral Ankle Sprain Management and Rehabilitation

Lateral ankle sprains represent over 80% of all sports-related ankle injuries, commonly involving the anterior talofibular ligament (ATFL) and calcaneofibular ligament (CFL).

## Ottawa Ankle Rules (Fracture Screening)
Obtain an X-ray if there is pain in the malleolar zone AND any of:
1. Bone tenderness along the posterior edge or tip of lateral malleolus (distal 6 cm).
2. Bone tenderness along the posterior edge or tip of medial malleolus (distal 6 cm).
3. Inability to bear weight both immediately after injury and for 4 consecutive steps in clinic.
If any rule is positive, refer immediately to urgent care or an orthopedist.

## Rehabilitation Phases
1. **Phase 1 (Days 1-4)**: Pain control, compression wrapping, gentle passive dorsiflexion/plantarflexion, alphabet tracing with toes.
2. **Phase 2 (Days 5-14)**: Resistance band eversion, calf raises (seated then standing), isometric peroneal contractions, single-leg balancing on firm surfaces.
3. **Phase 3 (Weeks 2-6)**: Wobble board balance, tandem stance eyes-closed, lateral hops, figure-eight agility runs, cutting mechanics.
4. **Return to Sport Criteria**: Full pain-free range of motion, Y-balance test symmetry >90% compared to contralateral limb, 20 consecutive single-leg hops without instability. Use supportive lace-up bracing for the first 6 months post-injury during competitive matches.

# Warm-Up and Movement Preparation Routines

## The RAMP Framework
Modern sports science recommends structuring pre-activity routines using the RAMP framework:
1. **Raise**: Elevate core body temperature, heart rate, respiration, and muscle elasticity with 5 minutes of light aerobic activity (jogging, cycling, skipping).
2. **Activate**: Fire key stabilizer muscle groups (glute bridges, lateral band walks, bird-dogs, rotator cuff activation).
3. **Mobilize**: Move major joints through full ranges of motion using dynamic stretching (hip openers, world's greatest stretch, leg swings, thoracic rotations). Never perform cold static stretching before power or sprint sports.
4. **Potentiate**: Prime the central nervous system with sport-specific high-intensity accelerations, plyometric jumps, and agility drills matching match speed.

## Sport-Specific Adjustments
- **Court Sports (Tennis, Badminton, Basketball)**: Emphasize lateral ankle stability, multi-directional lunges, and decelerations.
- **Field Sports (Football, Rugby)**: Emphasize hamstring eccentrics, groin openers, and high-speed linear sprinting.
- **Throwing/Overhead Sports**: Thorough scapular retraction, band pull-aparts, and sleeper stretches.
